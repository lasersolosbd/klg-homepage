Privacy Policy

Last updated October 12, 2026

This policy explains what information Kind Logic Group (“we,” “us”) collects when you use this website, write to us, or request an AI visibility report, how we use it, and the choices you have. We keep it short and plain on purpose.

Information we collect

  • Your name, work email address and, if you choose to give it, your mobile phone number.
  • Your organization’s name and website address, and anything you write in your message.
  • Your consent choices for text messages, including which boxes you checked and when.
  • If you request an AI visibility report: the public pages of your website we read and the answers AI assistants gave to questions about your organization.
  • Basic technical information such as your browser type and pages visited.

How we use it

  • To reply to your message and create your report if you asked for one.
  • To contact you about our services if you asked us to.
  • To send text messages only for the purposes you agreed to (see below).
  • To run, secure and improve this website and our service.

Text messages (SMS)

If you give us your mobile number and check a consent box on our form, we may text you as described on that form. Consent is optional and is not required to contact us or get a report. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out or HELP for help. More detail is in our Terms & Conditions.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with third parties for unrelated purposes. This does not include sharing with service providers that help us deliver messages or provide customer care, who may use the information only for those services.

Who else sees your information

We do not sell your personal information. We use service providers to run our website, store data, send email and text messages, and produce reports. They may access your information only to do that work for us. We may also disclose information if the law requires it or to protect our rights and the safety of others.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as needed to provide the service and meet legal obligations, then delete or anonymize it. You can ask us to delete it sooner.

Your choices

  • Reply STOP to any text to stop text messages.
  • Use the unsubscribe link in any marketing email.
  • Email us to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under state privacy laws.

Security and children

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information, but no system is perfectly secure. This service is for organizations and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes and contact

If we change this policy we will update the date above. Questions? Email hello@kindlogicgroup.com.

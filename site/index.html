Questions people ask.

Why is the visibility service one flat price?

Because the work is the same whether you’re a small shop or a large one. The AI tools don’t care about your budget, and neither does the check.

Is there a contract?

Month to month. The first month usually has the longest to-do list; after that it’s maintenance. Stop when it stops being useful.

What if we’re a small organization?

Run the free report anyway. It’s genuinely free and the fixes are yours to make. If you subscribe later, the price is the same.

We don’t have anyone technical. Is that a problem?

No. We build and maintain the systems. You need one person who can explain how your work really flows and approve what we make.

What happens with our donor data?

Before anything is built, we write down exactly which data a project touches. If a project doesn’t need donor records, it never sees them.

Will this replace our staff?

No. The goal is more reach for a small team. Every system keeps a person in the loop, and nothing goes out under your name without your review.
